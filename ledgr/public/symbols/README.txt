Symbol images bundled with Ledgr

crypto/  coin icons from cryptocurrency-icons 0.18.1 (CC0-1.0), see crypto/LICENSE.md
metals/  bullion icons drawn for Ledgr
flags/   country flags from flag-icons 7.6.0 (MIT), see flags/LICENSE

Company and index-owner logos are not bundled; /api/logo fetches them from
financialmodelingprep.com when a card is shown. Logos are trademarks of their owners
and are shown only to identify the instrument.
